Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Royal Sultan 1121 Kainat grain stretch to 20mm+?

▼
Our 1121 Kainat Basmati is selected from pedigreed seeds nurtured by Himalayan snowmelt waters and aged for up to 2 years in climate-controlled silos. This natural aging process gelatinizes starches, allowing each individual uncooked 8.35mm grain to double in length upon steaming without breaking or sticking.

How does the 1-Click WhatsApp order bot work?

▼
When you click "Order on WhatsApp", our platform generates a pre-formatted template with your selected rice variety, bag size, total price, and customer delivery city. You can send this in one tap to our automated dispatch team for instant confirmation.

What payment methods are supported for domestic orders?

▼
We support Cash on Delivery (COD) across 250+ cities in Pakistan, as well as direct mobile wallet payments via JazzCash, EasyPaisa, direct bank wire transfer, and online Visa/Mastercard payments.

Do you offer private labeling for international export containers?

▼
Yes, for 20ft and 40ft FCL bulk orders, we offer custom rotogravure printing, non-woven bags, and commercial jute sacks branded with your private trademark, complete with ISO 22000, Phytosanitary, and Halal certification paperwork.
